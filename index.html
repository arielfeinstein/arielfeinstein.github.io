I like learning new ideas and turning them into well-designed software—thinking through abstractions, tradeoffs, and edge cases until all the pieces fit together.

Who I am

I've been interested in computers since I was a kid, and that curiosity has only grown over time. When something looks intimidating, my usual response is “challenge accepted.” I learn best by getting my hands dirty—building things, breaking them, and figuring out why they broke.

What I study

I'm a fourth-year Computer Science student, currently participating in the Excellenteam program by Startup Nation Central.

My favorite courses have included Computability and Complexity, Advanced Object-Oriented Programming, Operating Systems, Real-Time Systems, and Computer Architecture. They taught me how software and computers work at different levels—from design patterns and C++ to memory management, file systems, process scheduling, and the relationship between CPU, RAM, and cache.

More recently, through Excellenteam's Python Backend course, I've been learning about microservice architecture and how to approach software development with production systems in mind.

Something I've built

I built Jumpster, a browser-based platform for creating, sharing, and playing custom 2D platformer levels. It includes a graphical level editor, platformer gameplay with enemies, coins, and checkpoints, user accounts, and a community feed for publishing and discovering levels.

Building it gave me experience connecting frontend game development with a full-stack backend and database.

What I want from this course

I want a better sense of what working in the industry actually feels like: collaborating with a team, working under pressure, and handling tasks in a realistic work environment.

I also want to develop practical tools for interviews and the job search. By the end of the bootcamp, I hope to feel more confident, better prepared, and equipped to find and succeed in my first job.
